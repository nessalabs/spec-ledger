# Show progress across all completion tasks

[Original spec](../spec.md) · [All fixups](../spec.md#fixups-and-amendments)

Correction date: 2026-09-06. [Change record](../../../../.spec-ledger/turns/259b685b-1331-4088-a691-d3fa16e57424.json).

## What needed correcting

Passing checks could show full progress while implementation, reviews or other completion tasks remained unfinished.

## What changed

Progress counts all applicable completion tasks. Evidence counts remain separate, and completed history remains distinct from readiness against current code.

## Evidence and history

Desktop, mobile, Overview and historical views were inspected, and regression checks were recorded with the original fixup.

This explanation was added on 2026-09-07 to make the earlier correction discoverable in the repository. The linked original record and its evidence have been preserved.
